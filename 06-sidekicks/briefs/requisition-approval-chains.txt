Requisition Approval Chains — one-pager
Product, Supply
14 July 2026

Problem

Right now, any requisition over any dollar amount goes through one
approval: a quartermaster signs off and it ships. For anything over
$2,000 — a new pack of hazmat-rated suits, a batch of grapple lines —
that's one person's judgment on a fairly large purchase, and Halloran's
asked more than once for a second set of eyes on the big-ticket items.

Proposal

Add a second approval step for requisitions over $2,000: a
quartermaster still approves first, then their manager signs off
before anything ships. That's the whole ask.

And while we're building the approval step, we should also let a
quartermaster route around a slow approver if they haven't responded
in 48 hours, and add a dashboard showing every pending approval across
all three sites so nobody has to ask around for status, and let a
handler see where their own requisition is stuck without going through
Halloran directly, and honestly — if we're building all of that, this
could eventually just replace the weekly gear-cage spreadsheet Halloran
keeps by hand.

Owner

Halloran's team, Supply.

Success measure

Fewer requisitions over $2,000 that ship without a second sign-off.
We'll know it's working when that number is zero.

Open questions

How much of the above ships together versus in phases — TBD.
