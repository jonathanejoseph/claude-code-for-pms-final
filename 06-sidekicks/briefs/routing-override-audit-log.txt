Routing Override Audit Log — one-pager
Product, Dispatch
5 March 2026

Problem

When a handler manually overrides Dispatch's routing — picks someone
other than the responder we recommended — there's no record of it
anywhere. No who, no when, no why. Nadia's team has hit this more than
once: a complaint comes in about a responder who was passed over, and
there's no way to even confirm an override happened, let alone why.

Proposal

Log every manual override: who made it, when, which responder they
picked instead of the recommended one, and a short reason if they
typed one. Surface it in the console's existing activity view, next to
the callout it belongs to.

Owner

Marcus's team builds it. Sofia has already mocked the activity-view
addition — it's a small addition to a screen that exists.

Scope

Overrides only. Not a general audit log for every action in the
console — just the one decision that currently leaves no trace.

Next steps

Marcus's team can pick this up once profile redesign wraps. Small,
contained, ready to build.
