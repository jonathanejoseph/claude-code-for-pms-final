Handler Phone App — one-pager
Product, Dispatch
2 August 2026

Proposal

We should build a phone app for handlers. Right now the console is
desktop-only, which means a handler is tied to their desk to see
anything about their responder. A phone app would give handlers push
notifications the moment something changes, a live view of their
responder's current status, and one-tap contact straight from the
notification — call or message, no need to open the console first.

What this solves

Aunt Dot — a handler in the interview pile — described finding out her
responder had declined a callout only when she happened to glance at
the console later that evening. A phone app would put that same
information in front of her the moment it happens, instead of whenever
she next happens to look.

Owner

Sofia, exploring for Q4 alongside her console work. Not yet staffed
for a build.

Success measure

Fewer handlers reporting they missed a status change because they
weren't at the console. No baseline number yet — that's the metric to
start tracking once this moves past exploration.

Scope

Just the three things above: notifications, status view, one-tap
contact. Nothing from Supply, nothing from the console's activity log
— a phone app that mirrors the console's other features is a second
project, not this one.
